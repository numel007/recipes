# Zuppa Toscana

## Ingredients

| Name              | Quantity |   Configuration    |
| ----------------- | -------- | :----------------: |
| Hot sausage       | 1 lb      |         -          |
| Diced bacon             | 8 oz    |      -       |
| Yellow onions     | 1-2 large  |       Diced        |
| Russet potatoes   | 3-4      | Sliced into 1/4 inch rounds, skin on |
| Spinach/Kale           | 4 cups   |         -          |
| Chicken stock     | 64 oz    |         -          |
| Heavy cream       | 2 cups    |         -          |
| Red pepper flakes | 1 tbsp   |         -          |
| Garlic            | 4 cloves |       Minced       |
|Table salt|To taste|-|
|Ground black pepper|To taste|-|

_Yield: ~6 servings_

## Method

1. Brown sausage in 5 qt pot. Add in diced bacon and cook until everything is browned.
1. Add red pepper flakes and toast until fragrant.
1. Add `onions` and `garlic`. Saute until softened, scraping the fond off the bottom of the pot.
1. Add sliced potatoes, chicken stock, 2 tsp table salt, and ground black pepper. Bring to a low simmer.
2. Cook 10-15 mins, or until potatoes are just done.
1. Add `spinach/kale` and simmer 1-2 mins, or until soft.
1. Turn off heat and allow soup to come to a still, ie no longer bubbling.
1. Stir in heavy cream. Adding cream while soup is boiling can cause curdling.
1. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
